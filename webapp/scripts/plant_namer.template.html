Plant spot namer

Name and review the circles, then export. Export saves the named spots only, so maps can be done one at a time; unnamed proposals are left out.

Drag a circle to move it; drag its edge to resize. Add places a circle at your next map click. Make rectangle keeps the circle's size; drag a selected rectangle's square handles to resize each side or corner, and its round handle to rotate. Fit to plants makes a rectangle around the plants inside the selected shape. Merge keeps the selected spot's name and covers both circles. Undo reverses one edit, including a whole drag. Edits stay in this tab until export; reloading restores this page's starting data.

    Selected circle

    U, V and radius use the original minimap's 0–10000 units. The displayed rotation never changes stored coordinates.
