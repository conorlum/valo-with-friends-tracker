Tool

Erase clears the paint kind you painted last (see-across). See-across: black you can see over (drops, gaps). Cover: blocks sight and walking. Can't walk: floor nobody can stand on; sight is unchanged. Uncertain: a note where the flat map may be wrong; it changes nothing. Barrier: the buy-phase barrier lines. Press, drag and release to draw one straight, thin line right across each gap, touching the walls at both ends; when they drop, each team holds its side of them. Sight and walking are unchanged.
