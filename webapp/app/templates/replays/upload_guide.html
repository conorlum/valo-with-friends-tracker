How to upload your Valorant replays

Step 1: Download the replay in Valorant

Open the Career page in Valorant and find the match under Match History. Click the download arrow on the right side of the match.

A match is downloaded once the arrow turns into a play button.

Step 2: Open the upload page

  1. Open your browser to the upload link: upload page
  2. Put in the code: {{ invite_code }}

Step 3: Go to your replay folder

  1. Hit the upload button on the page. A file window opens.
  2. Click the empty part of the address bar at the top, shown in green below. It turns into a text box.
  3. Paste in this and press Enter: %LOCALAPPDATA%\VALORANT\Saved\Demos

You should now see your replay files. They end in .vrf.

Step 4: Pick your replays

Only 5 at a time please!

  1. Click the "Date modified" column header so the most recent replays are at the top.
  2. Click the first replay you want, then hold Shift and click the last one. Everything in between gets selected.
  3. Hit Open.

Step 5: Upload and wait

  1. Click upload on the browser page.
  2. Keep the page open until every replay says it is done uploading.
  3. Once a replay says "parsing", your side is done and you can close the page.

Then you are done!
